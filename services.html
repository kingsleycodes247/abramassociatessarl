What We Do

Services built around one goal: delivery.

Every service we offer exists to remove a specific point of risk from a cross-border trade — physical or digital.

Service 01

Sourcing & Procurement

We work with vetted mining, smelting and refining partners across Central and West Africa to source copper, aluminium, bauxite and lead to buyer specification — including grade, volume and delivery timeline.

Service 02

Quality Assurance & Inspection

Independent third-party inspection and sampling is arranged ahead of every shipment, with certificates of analysis issued before goods are released for loading.

Service 03

Logistics & Export Documentation

Customs clearance, freight forwarding, bills of lading and certificates of origin are handled end-to-end through the Port of Douala — Central Africa's principal maritime gateway.

Service 04

Trade Finance & Contract Structuring

We structure contracts against letters of credit, telegraphic transfer or escrow terms, balancing risk between buyer and seller on every deal.

Service 05

Digital Asset (OTC) Brokerage

Our OTC desk executes block trades in major crypto tokens and stablecoins for counterparties who prefer digital settlement or treasury diversification, subject to KYC/AML checks.

Service 06

Dedicated Relationship Management

Every contract is assigned a single relationship manager who stays accountable from first inquiry through final delivery and settlement.
