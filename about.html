Company Profile

Built in Bonapriso.
Trading toward the world.

Abram Associates SARL is a registered trading company operating from Bonapriso, Douala — Cameroon's commercial capital and the location of Central Africa's busiest port.

Who We Are

A trading house, not a broker.

Abram Associates SARL sources, verifies and exports raw materials and mineral resources — copper, aluminium, bauxite and lead — from Central Africa to buyers across the globe. We take positions as principal or structured intermediary depending on the contract, and we hold ourselves to the same documentation standard on every deal, large or small.

Alongside our physical trade desk, we operate an over-the-counter digital asset trading arm, allowing counterparties to diversify settlement between fiat, letters of credit, and major crypto tokens where appropriate.
