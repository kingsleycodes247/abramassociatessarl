=====================================
Until you upload real images, the website automatically shows a
labelled placeholder in the exact size needed, so the layout looks
correct either way. Design each file in Canva at the dimensions
below, export as JPG (photos) or PNG (logo/favicon, for transparency),
and upload it to the /images/ folder using the EXACT filename listed.
The site will pick it up automatically — no code changes needed.

TIPS
- Keep product photos on a clean, dark or neutral background so the
  copper/bronze accent colour in the site stands out against them.
- The overall palette is: anthracite black, copper/bronze, warm
  aluminium grey, and an ochre red (bauxite). Keep photo edits (Canva
  filters) subtle and slightly desaturated rather than bright/glossy —
  it should read as "serious trading house," not "stock photo agency."
- If you don't have real product photography yet, well-licensed stock
  photos of raw copper, aluminium ingots, bauxite ore and lead ingots
  work fine as a placeholder until real photos are available.
